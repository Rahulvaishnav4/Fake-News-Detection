How Verification Works

Our platform does not rely on opaque guessing. Instead, it grounds every assessment in real-time online corroboration, domain reliability ratings, and transparent evidence synthesis.

1

User Submits Claim or Headline

You enter a headline, article text, viral rumor, or direct web link.

2

System Queries Live Google News Index

The engine queries real-time Google News RSS endpoints and search indexes to retrieve matching and related news reports from current journalism.

3

Entity & Claim Proposition Extraction

Extracts key factual entities, dates, numbers, and core propositions while filtering out search noise.

4

Multi-Source Cross-Corroboration

Evaluates whether independent, reputable news organizations (Reuters, AP, BBC, The Hindu, Indian Express, PTI, etc.) are reporting the same event.

5

Linguistic & Credibility Scoring

Calculates clickbait and sensationalism indexes, flags hyperbolic shouting or conspiracy terminology, and checks our registry for known satire or unreliable websites.

6

Evidence Synthesis & Assessment

Synthesizes the weighted signals into an objective verdict: 🟢 Likely REAL, 🔴 Likely FAKE, or 🟡 UNCERTAIN / NEEDS VERIFICATION.

7

Transparent Evidence Report Delivered

You receive a transparent breakdown containing exact citations, sources checked, confidence score, and supporting/contradicting evidence.

Ethical & Assistive AI Principles

The system strictly avoids arbitrary declarations of truth. If insufficient evidence exists or reputable reporting has not yet caught up with breaking events, the system will explicitly return UNCERTAIN / NEEDS VERIFICATION. Automated assessments are designed to empower your human critical thinking, not replace it.

About Fake News Detection System

The Fake News Detection System is an advanced, automated journalism verification platform built to combat the rampant spread of misinformation, deceptive clickbait, and fabricated stories online.

Our Architecture

Unlike shallow machine learning models that guess based only on the raw words of a sentence, our system employs a multi-signal verification framework:

  • Real-Time Corroboration: Interrogates live Google News feeds to ascertain if mainstream news agencies are reporting the event.
  • Domain Trust Hierarchy: Maintains indexed credibility scores for Tier-1 news organizations, specialized trade publications, certified fact-checkers, and satire sites.
  • Linguistic Stylistics: Analyzes text for emotional manipulation, sensationalist clickbait phrasing, excessive capitalization, and punctuation anomalies.
  • Grounded Caution: Never assumes truth in the absence of evidence; defaults to UNCERTAIN / NEEDS VERIFICATION whenever corroboration is insufficient.
